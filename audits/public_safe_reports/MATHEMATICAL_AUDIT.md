# Mathematical audit

The single-assessment proof distinguishes positivity of the theory conjoined with the candidate from unconditional term mass. The assessed event is satisfiable in the ambient valuation space, lies outside the theory, implies every positive selector term, and excludes the valid fallback. Consistent partial assignments have residual-mass witnesses. A full existential witness induces a strict rival, and any successful partial assignment extends to such a witness. Every term containing s stays below or ties the fallback in every measure.

The listed-support proof gives distinct complete valuations with rational masses summing to one. The unique coherent distribution retains 2n−m selector points for a nonempty term with m selected literals. The empty term fails entailment, as does every term missing a selector pair. A better solution must have exactly one selector per pair. The observation's guards make every other extension satisfy it, leaving all universal assignments for the induced existential assignment.

For constant maximum support size the common-literal conjunction of a qualifying support subset strengthens every term true on that subset. Failure of entailment on each such conjunction has a countervaluation certificate. The two-point examples keep the independent SAT and UNSAT conditions separate. The replays are exact finite diagnostics.
