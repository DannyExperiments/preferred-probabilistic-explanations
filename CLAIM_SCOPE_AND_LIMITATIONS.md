# Scope and limitations

The recognition target is a designated term in the PrAP model of Definitions 23 and 24. A solution must entail the observation together with the theory and have positive probability jointly with the theory for some coherent measure. The preference comparison uses unconditional term mass and is evaluated rival by rival.

The single-assessment proof uses an assessed event disjoint from the theory. The explicit-support proof independently uses a tautological theory and a unique measure represented by its listed positive points. Every listed point satisfies the observation, but entailment still ranges over all valuations. Constant support bounds refer to at most k positive support points for fixed k. The two hardness examples preserve valid candidates when the theorem says so.

The finite replay tests representative inputs. External peer review, formal verification and an exhaustive priority search remain pending.
